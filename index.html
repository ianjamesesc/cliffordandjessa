GENTLE REMINDERS & FAQs

Notes for Our Guests

What time should I arrive?

We’d love for you to be comfortably seated before the ceremony begins! To help everything go smoothly and allow everyone to settle in, we kindly recommend arriving at least one hour before the ceremony. We can’t wait to share this special moment with you!

Can I bring someone else to your wedding with me?

We’re so happy to celebrate this special day with you! However, due to our limited venue capacity, we’re only able to accommodate the guests included on our guest list. Each seat has been thoughtfully reserved for a specific guest, so we kindly ask that you join us only if your name is on the invitation. Thank you so much for understanding and for being part of our special day.

📵 UNPLUGGED CEREMONY

A Little Request from our Hearts

As we begin this beautiful new chapter of our lives, we hope to share our wedding ceremony with all of you in the most meaningful way. We wish to keep this special moment solemn and intimate, allowing us to be fully present as we exchange our vows and become husband and wife.

With love, we kindly ask everyone to refrain from taking photos or videos during the ceremony. Please leave the capturing of these precious moments to our wonderful photography and videography team.

Once we are announced as husband and wife, feel free to take out your phones, snap photos, and celebrate this special moment with us!

Don't worry, we'll make sure to share our wedding photos with you after the celebration so we can all look back on these beautiful memories together.

Thank you for understanding and for being part of one of the most meaningful days of our lives. Your presence means so much to us, and we can't wait to celebrate this love with you!
